About Us

Welcome

Founded on June 20, 1977, under decree no. 77-92, the Blida University Center commenced its academic activities on September 8, 1981, enrolling 526 students and employing a faculty of 57, including 17 international scholars. Subsequently, by executive decree no. 89-137 dated August 1, 1989, the institution was elevated to university status.

The cooperative program at Blida 1 University serves as a vital training approach, facilitating the progressive integration of young university students into an ever-evolving and competitive job market.Annually, approximately 5,000 cooperative internships are conducted across a diverse range of 1,000 organizations.Consequently, the University is ranked among the top 10 higher education institutions in Algeria.

Word from the Rector

Prof. MOHAMED BEZZINA

"Blida 1 University, named after the esteemed nationalist Saad Dahlab, emerged from the transformation of the city’s first university center into a full-fledged university in 1989. Situated in a region rich in both agricultural and industrial potential, it comprises 4 faculties and 4 institutes, offering specialized training in well-defined academic fields. The university strives to align its programs with the socio-economic demands of the Mitidja region and beyond. It delivers high-quality education at the License, Master, and Doctorate levels, supported by experienced faculty members and dedicated administrative staff.

With approximately thirty research laboratories, the university fosters continuous development, where researchers focus on key national priorities while engaging with contemporary scientific topics. Its commitment to openness is reflected in numerous cooperation agreements with national institutions and international academic programs."

Why choose us

Blida 1 University offers high-quality and diverse programs designed to enrich your knowledge, enhance your skills, and equip you with the expertise needed to thrive in both the job market and society. Our graduates achieve remarkable success, with an employment rate exceeding 80%—some even securing jobs before completing their studies! Beyond academics, Blida 1 provides a welcoming environment and a balanced, nature-connected lifestyle. Studying here means immersing yourself in a vibrant and enriching experience. Nestled near the mountains, our university campus boasts a refreshing and inspiring setting, where students can enjoy outdoor activities, top-tier sports facilities, and engaging cultural events, ensuring an exciting and fulfilling student life.
