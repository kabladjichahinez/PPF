Our Services

Preliminary statistical survey 01

compiling annual data on student enrollment, postgraduate admissions, educational infrastructure, and faculty records to meet the requirements of the supervisory authority (MESRS).

Overall census of infrastructure at the University of Blida 1

listing and measuring all educational and administrative spaces to create a comprehensive database on the university’s infrastructure and teaching capacity.

Progress Report

conducting an evolutionary study on the growth of faculties, departments, and options at the University of Blida since 1981, covering student demographics, faculty data, and infrastructure statistics.

Statistical survey 02 final

preparing a final statistical report on the university, covering student enrollment, postgraduate graduates, faculty members, and administrative and technical staff.

Equipment and Construction

This service involves developing and implementing equipment and construction programs for BLIDA 1 and BLIDA 2 universities, studying construction contracts, and overseeing project execution and monitoring.
